Garden Gym Wall Sheet

30 minutes · Mon / Wed / Fri, alternating A-B-A then B-A-B · Rest 60–90s between sets
Warm-up (every session, 5 min): 2 min easy spin bike · 10 slow bodyweight squats · 8 push-ups · 10 band pull-aparts · 5 arm circles each way

Session A — Squat & Push

ExerciseSets × Reps
1. Goblet Squat Kettlebell hugged to chest · hips back · heels down · chest up3 × 8–12
2. Dips (bent knee) Shoulders away from ears · slow down, smooth up · band under knees if needed3 × 6–10
3. Overhead Press Bar at collarbones · squeeze glutes · press to straight arms · no lean-back3 × 6–10
4. Romanian Deadlift Hips straight back · bar brushes thighs to mid-shin · flat back always3 × 10–12
5. Hanging Knee Raise No swinging · knees to hip height · 2 seconds down2 × 8–12

Session B — Pull & Hinge

ExerciseSets × Reps
1. Pull-Up Dead hang start · chest to bar · 2–3s lowering · band for extra reps3 × 4–8
2. Bent-Over Row Hinge to 45° · pull bar to belly button · torso stays still3 × 8–12
3. Reverse Lunge Kettlebell at chest · step back · front thigh level · push through front heel3 × 8–10 /leg
4. Floor Press Upper arms rest lightly on floor · pause · press · forearms vertical3 × 8–12
5. Suitcase Carry One-hand carry · shoulders level · slow steady lines2 × 30–40s /side
