Guide

The week

Mon / Wed / Fri, alternating: A-B-A one week, B-A-B the next. The home screen keeps track and suggests the right one. The rest days between are part of the plan — the science says training the same muscles more often adds nothing once weekly sets are equal, and recovery is where the growth happens.

Optional: Saturday Extras — a light day of carries, knee raises and bike intervals. A bonus, never a requirement.

Effort

End every set feeling like you had 1–2 clean reps left. Finish fresh — too light. Form broke — too heavy. The set ends when form breaks, not when the number says so.

Feeling cold or stiff? Do your first bar-lift set light — half the weight, easy reps — then start counting.

The volume target

The research benchmark: about ten hard sets per muscle, per week, spread across at least two sessions. Hard sets are the unit that counts — doing more reps inside a set, or spreading the same sets over more days, doesn’t add growth. The app ramps you there automatically — two sets per exercise for your first couple of sessions, three after that, and a fourth set unlocks on the main lifts once you’ve banked eight sessions of a type without it feeling like too much. At full ramp you’re in that ten-set zone.

Rest between sets

90 seconds by default, 60 on core and carries. The research floor is 60 seconds — shorter starts costing growth; much longer mainly helps very heavy lifting. The timer chimes when it’s done, through your AirPods if they’re in.

Stretching

Honest science: stretching won’t prevent soreness — but a few minutes most days genuinely improves how freely you move within a few weeks. The Stretch list is on the home screen; any evening works.

Progress

The app suggests adding 1.25 kg only when you hit the top of the rep range on every set and the session didn’t feel like too much. If a session felt like too much, it tells you to repeat the same weights — that’s progress too.

Apple Watch

Start each session in the built-in Workout app on “Traditional Strength Training” for heart rate and rings. Log sets here. If you ever want wrist logging, export your CSV and import into an app like Hevy.

Form checks

Record whole sessions in Elgato Camera Hub — hit record just before tapping Begin so the app’s set times line up with the video. Afterwards, tell Claude Code “process video” in the garden-gym repo: it finds the recording, pulls frames of your working sets, critiques your form, and keeps a running notes file. Once a week is plenty.
